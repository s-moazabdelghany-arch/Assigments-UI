About the event

Campus Tech Day is a student-focused event with short talks, project displays, and demonstrations. It is designed for students who want to share ideas, meet other learners, and explore technology in a friendly setting.

Date: 15 November 2026
Time: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM
Venue: University Main Hall
Entry: Free for university students. Please register so the organisers can estimate attendance.

Event agenda

  1. 10:00 AM – Check-in and welcome
  2. 10:30 AM – Student project showcase
  3. 12:00 PM – Short technology talks
  4. 1:00 PM – Break and networking
  5. 2:00 PM – Demonstrations and closing
